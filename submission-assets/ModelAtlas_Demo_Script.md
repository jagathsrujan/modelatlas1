# ModelAtlas - CodeFury 9.0 Demo Video Script

Target length: 5 to 7 minutes

Record the final version from the hosted website. Use `?demo=true` for the reliable seeded flow if live provider keys are not required for the judging demonstration. Keep the browser address bar visible briefly at the start so judges can identify the hosted application, then use full-screen capture for the product walkthrough.

## Opening - 0:00 to 0:25

On screen: ModelAtlas landing page.

Narration:

"AI teams do not start with a model name. They start with a workload, constraints, and a decision. ModelAtlas is the decision layer before you spend. It helps a non-specialist move from plain-language work to a privacy-safe, evidence-backed AI infrastructure plan."

Show the landing-page animation, the two entry paths, the Define / Verify / Compare / Plan sequence, and the privacy/evidence trust signals.

## Personal Explorer intake - 0:25 to 1:10

Action:

1. Open Personal Explorer.
2. Use the seeded scenario for invoices, spreadsheets, and product images in Pune.
3. Show the extracted goal, inputs, volume, budget, country, horizon, and user count.
4. Point out that the user can edit the extracted facts before continuing.

Narration:

"The user can describe the work without knowing model or infrastructure terminology. ModelAtlas extracts a structured workload profile and makes the assumptions visible before it ranks anything."

## Privacy hard filter - 1:10 to 1:40

Action:

1. Choose Confidential.
2. Show that the summary changes to self-hosted or private-cloud hosting and excludes external APIs.

Narration:

"Privacy is a hard filter, not a ranking preference. Confidential work removes ineligible external APIs before the platform compares the remaining options."

## Hardware verification - 1:40 to 2:25

Action:

1. Open hardware verification.
2. Show the seeded Mac Studio, RTX 4090, MacBook, and DGX Spark evidence tasks.
3. Open an evidence drawer.
4. Show field confidence, source details, review, edit, and save behavior.

Narration:

"The recommendation is grounded in the hardware the organization already owns. Evidence is extracted field by field, confidence is visible, and the user can review or correct it before it becomes reusable inventory."

## Recommendation and cost comparison - 2:25 to 3:35

Action:

1. Select Privacy / Local-First.
2. Show the primary recommendation and why it fits.
3. Open Costs and show hardware, electricity, operations, and usage assumptions separately.
4. Open Alternatives and show excluded candidates and the VRAM-not-pooled warning.
5. Open Verification and show source, URL, timestamp, and confidence.

Narration:

"ModelAtlas recommends the simplest sufficient strategy. It explains the model family, hosting, hardware, setup, confidence, assumptions, alternatives, risks, and verification work. Cost lines stay separate so the result is auditable instead of looking like a single unexplained price."

## Research Scout - 3:35 to 4:05

Action:

1. Open Research Scout.
2. Show Official, Benchmarks, Community, and Procurement evidence.
3. Show the source links, evidence tier, timestamp, confidence, and community warning.

Narration:

"When current information matters, Research Scout keeps the evidence traceable. Official and benchmark sources are separated from community signals, which remain clearly labeled until corroborated."

## Team workspace - 4:05 to 4:55

Action:

1. Convert the decision into the seeded team workspace.
2. Show Finance, Operations, and Support role profiles.
3. Show the aggregated Shared Document Intelligence opportunity.
4. Open Members and show private-by-default sharing controls.

Narration:

"A personal decision can become a team opportunity without exposing individual profiling. Role context stays private by default, while only useful aggregated workload insight moves into the workspace."

## Implementation plan and policies - 4:55 to 5:45

Action:

1. Open Inventory and show selected hardware and cluster guidance.
2. Open the implementation plan.
3. Show the RAG rationale, architecture, costs, phases, risks, and verification.
4. Open Policies and show allowlists, hosting restrictions, and the hard-filter preview.

Narration:

"The outcome is a plan, not a cart. ModelAtlas turns the recommendation into architecture, delivery phases, risks, approvals, and policy controls that a team can act on."

## Assistant and close - 5:45 to 6:20

Action:

1. Open Ask ModelAtlas.
2. Ask: `Why Privacy / Local-First?`
3. Show the concise answer and its trust label.
4. Return to the recommendation or landing page.

Narration:

"The assistant explains the same deterministic decision logic in plain language. It cannot override privacy policy, invent prices, or pretend that incompatible hardware pools its VRAM. ModelAtlas connects discovery, trust, cost, and deployment planning before an organization spends."

## Recording checklist

- Use the hosted URL, not localhost.
- Use `?demo=true` if the seeded offline path is part of the intended submission.
- Keep the cursor visible when clicking important controls.
- Pause briefly on privacy, evidence, cost, Scout, workspace, and plan screens.
- Do not show API keys, `.env` files, private Supabase values, or personal credentials.
- Export as MP4 at 1080p if possible.
- Upload to YouTube as Unlisted or Google Drive with link access enabled.
- Test the public link in a private browser window before submitting it.
